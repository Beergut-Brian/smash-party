Smash Party cars use this pack as-is. It is CC0 (public domain): free for any use, and credit is not required.

| Pack | What we use | License |
|---|---|---|
| [Kenney Car Kit 3.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) | The driveable cars in `assets/cars/` | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| [Kenney City Kit (Suburban)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-suburban) | Houses, cottages, villas, farmhouses (`assets/props/suburban-*.glb`) | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| [Kenney City Kit (Commercial)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial) | Shops and the tall town building (`commercial-*.glb`) | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| [Kenney City Kit (Roads)](https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-roads) | Street lamps, traffic cones | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| [Kenney Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) | Trees, bushes, flowers, mushrooms, pebbles, fences, crops, melons | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| [Kenney Fantasy Town Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit) | Park fountain, windmill sails | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| [Kenney Holiday Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit) | Park benches, snowmen | [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |

World props in `assets/props/` are baked from those packs by `assets/props/bake.mjs` (merged into one mesh, texture colors turned into vertex colors, Nature Kit recolored to the game's palette). The block-built props in `src/objects.js` are original.

Car names in the game are original. The block-built cars in `src/cars.js` are original too.
